Callout Rush - booth build (no internet needed after unzip)
1. Install Node.js 18 or newer (https://nodejs.org). Nothing else.
2. Double-click start.command (Mac) or start.bat (Windows). Or run: node serve.mjs
3. The game opens at http://localhost:4173/  (press F for full screen).
4. Open the second window at http://localhost:4173/board.html and drag it to the second monitor.
5. Stop with Ctrl+C in the terminal window.
